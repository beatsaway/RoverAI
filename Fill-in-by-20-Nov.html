As 9GK homeroom teacher I set up consistent routines for morning devotion, attendance, homework and class meetings, because a predictable start to the day makes everything else easier. I tried to know each student well enough to notice when something had changed, and to follow up quietly rather than waiting for a problem to become visible. When students had difficulties — with work, with each other, or at home — I spoke with them individually, involved the counsellor or Year Level Head where needed, and kept parents informed so that home and school were responding in the same way. I also worked on class identity: students took on class responsibilities, we dealt with conflict openly, and I tried to make it clear that the class was something they were building together rather than something imposed on them. Attendance and punctuality were followed up consistently. The area I most want to improve is communication with parents — I would like to make contact earlier and more regularly, including when things are going well, rather than mainly when there is an issue. Next year I plan to set a simple schedule for parent contact so that it does not depend on finding time.
